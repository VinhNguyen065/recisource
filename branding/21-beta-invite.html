A food, health & fitness app — scan any meal with your camera to count calories, import recipes from any link, plan meals, and track workouts with live sensors.
vinhnguyen065.github.io/recisource

Get set up (1 minute)

  1. Scan the QR with your phone camera (or type the link into Safari / Chrome).
  2. Add it to your home screen so it feels like a real app:
    iPhone: Share button → Add to Home Screen
    Android: ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen
  3. Create a free account (or tap "Skip for now" to just look around). All plans are free during the beta — no card, ever.
  4. Try the fun stuff: scan a meal with the camera, paste a recipe link (YouTube works!), start a live workout, measure your pulse with the camera.

What to tell us

  1. Anything confusing, broken, or stuck.
  2. Was the calorie estimate believable?
  3. What made you smile — and what would make you open it daily?
Beta notes: it's a work in progress and updates itself automatically. Your data is private to your account. Camera, motion and mic features need you to tap "Allow" when your phone asks.
