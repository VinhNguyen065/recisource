Common questions

The camera does not open when I scan a meal

Open Settings → 21again on your iPhone and make sure Camera is switched on. In the app, tap Scan a meal → Take a photo. You can also choose Photo library or file to use a picture you already took, or describe the meal in words instead.

The calories look wrong

Scan results are estimates. Before you log, use − / + on any item to correct the portion, or add a missed item by hand. Rows marked Portion is a guess are the ones most worth checking. Good light and the whole plate in frame make a real difference.

Apple Health is not filling in my steps, sleep or heart rate

Open the Health app → your profile picture → Apps → 21again and turn on the categories you want to share. 21again only reads what you allow, and everything still works with manual entry if you prefer not to connect it.

Do I need an account?

No. 21again works without one and keeps your log on your device. An account is only needed if you want your data synced to another device.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and synced data from our servers. To clear data stored only on your device, use Settings → Start fresh, or delete the app. You can take a full copy first with Settings → Export my data.

Is 21again medical advice?

No. 21again provides general wellness information and estimates only. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition. Please talk to a qualified professional before changing your diet, exercise or medication.
