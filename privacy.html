Privacy Policy

21again · Last updated 30 August 2026

21again ("we", "us", the "app") helps you scan meals, track food, health and fitness, plan recipes and set reminders. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We designed 21again to keep as much on your device as possible.

Information we collect

  • Account details. If you create an account, we store your email address and an encrypted password to sync your data across your devices. You can use the app without an account, in which case no email is collected.
  • Your logged data. Food entries, weight, steps, heart-rate readings, blood pressure, sleep, workouts, recipes, meal plans, grocery lists, reminders and any medical conditions or medications you choose to add. This is data you enter.
  • Photos you scan. When you scan a meal, product or recipe, the photo is sent to our processing service to estimate its contents. Photos are used only to return that result and are not stored for advertising or profiling.
  • Health data (iOS). With your permission, the app can read steps, heart rate and sleep from Apple Health to display on your dashboard. This data stays on your device and is not sold or shared.
  • Device sensors. With your permission, the app uses motion (to count steps and reps), the camera (to scan meals and measure pulse), and the microphone (for hands-free clap/voice control during workouts). Camera and microphone streams are processed on your device in real time and are not recorded or uploaded, except a meal photo you explicitly scan.

How we use it

  • To provide the app's features — counting calories, tracking trends, planning meals, and firing the reminders you set.
  • To sync your data to your private account so it follows you to a new device.
  • To generate AI results (calorie estimates, recipe imports, assistant replies) by sending the relevant text or photo to our AI provider.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Service providers

We share the minimum data needed with:

  • Supabase — hosts your account and synced data (authentication and encrypted-at-rest database).
  • Anthropic — processes scan photos, recipe text/links and assistant messages to return AI results. Content is sent for processing and is not used to identify you.
  • Apple — App Store distribution and, if you enable it, Apple Health.

These providers act as processors under our instructions and their own security and privacy commitments.

Data retention & deletion

Your on-device data stays until you clear it. If you have an account, your synced data is kept until you delete it. You can:

  • Delete your account in the app — Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and synced data from our servers.
  • Export a full backup — Settings → Export my data — at any time.
  • Start fresh — Settings → Start fresh — to clear sample and tracked data on the device.

Children

21again is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Security

Accounts are protected by password authentication and data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. No system is perfectly secure; please use a strong, unique password.

Health disclaimer

21again provides general wellness information and estimates only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Always consult a qualified professional before changing your diet, exercise or medication.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your personal data. The in-app Export and Delete tools cover most of these; for anything else, contact us.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be noted with a new "last updated" date. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
